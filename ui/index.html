Video & audio engine

FFmpeg handles video and audio conversion on your device. Use an installation on PATH, or choose the executable directly.

Checking FFmpeg…

Windows: install a full FFmpeg build, then choose ffmpeg.exe from its bin folder. FFprobe is optional. Photos work independently of FFmpeg.
